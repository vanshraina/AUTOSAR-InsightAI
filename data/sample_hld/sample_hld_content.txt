1. System overview
The Vehicle Dynamics domain provides vehicle-speed information to braking and driver-assistance functions.
VehicleSpeedService publishes VehicleSpeedSignal through VehicleSpeedInterface. BrakeController consumes
VehicleSpeedSignal and depends on VehicleSpeedService. The signal is sampled by the vehicle dynamics layer.

2. Cabin monitoring
CabinMonitor reads CabinTemperatureSignal and DoorStatusSignal. CabinMonitor reports cabin status to
VehicleStatusManager through CabinStatusInterface. VehicleStatusManager coordinates status reporting for the
vehicle display. This example is illustrative only and is not a normative AUTOSAR design.

3. Interface summary
VehicleSpeedInterface: producer VehicleSpeedService; consumer BrakeController; payload VehicleSpeedSignal.
CabinStatusInterface: producer CabinMonitor; consumer VehicleStatusManager; payload CabinTemperatureSignal
and DoorStatusSignal.
